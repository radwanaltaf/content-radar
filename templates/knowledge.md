# Internal knowledge pack

What this project knows that cannot be found on another website.

**This is the highest-value file in the pipeline.** A drafting step without it
is working from the same public corpus as the pages it is trying to beat, so it
produces a version of them. That is precisely what a search engine treats as
unhelpful content made to rank. Every article the radar produces must carry at
least one thing from here.

Two rules for adding to it. It has to be true, and it has to be yours. A figure
copied from an industry report belongs in the article as a citation with a link,
not in this file.

---

## Proof we can actually show

Named clients, real figures, what is publishable about each and what is not.
Be precise about the relationship: delivered directly, through an employer, or
as part of a larger programme are three different claims.

## What we will never claim

Absolute, and it overrides any instruction to the contrary including one in a
prompt.

- No client, testimonial, logo or result that is not real.
- No invented statistic, including a plausible one.
- No claim about how often something happens in the market without a citation
  and a link. "Most companies" is a judgement and can be written as one.
  "73% of companies" is a claim and needs a source.
